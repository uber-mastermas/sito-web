The Sanctuary Milan

An industrial space reimagined as a living sanctuary.

The Sanctuary Milan is a stage for creativity, connection and transformation, where every detail invites you to slow down and feel part of something greater.

Restaurant & bar

Restaurant

Mediterranean flair, Asian touches and a menu made to be shared.

Mediterranean roots meet tropical and Asian influences in a cuisine made to be shared: a gastronomic experience where atmosphere is part of the recipe.

Local products, innovative culinary techniques and the distinctive Sanctuary taste, reflected in every plate and every moment. After dinner, an exotic bar for those who are not looking for realism, but magic.

Garden & Pool Area · Summer season

A city escape

Somewhere between a city escape and a summer ritual.

Hidden within lush gardens, the Pool Area offers a rare escape from the rhythm of the city: sunbeds, shaded lounge spaces and an outdoor bar serving fresh juices, signature cocktails and an all-day à la carte menu.

Here the day unfolds slowly between the pool, the garden and the sun.

Club · Thursday to Sunday

Internationally renowned artists, who meet in one magical place.
