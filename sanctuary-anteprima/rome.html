The Sanctuary Rome

Located in the heart of Rome, just steps away from the Colosseum.

In the historic area of Colle Oppio and the Baths of Titus and Trajan, nestled in the Monti district, famous for its art galleries, wine bars, artisan shops and gourmet restaurants: the go-to destination for a young, trend-savvy crowd.

Restaurant & bar

Restaurant

Rooted in Mediterranean tradition and local ingredients, every dish evolves into a sensory journey spanning distant cultures.

A culinary exploration that harmoniously blends tropical and Asian influences, creating a unique gastronomic ritual.

Every ingredient is carefully selected, every flavor evokes faraway lands, creating an experience that transcends borders and celebrates the fusion of different cultures in a boundless cuisine.
