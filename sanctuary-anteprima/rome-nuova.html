The Sanctuary Rome

Between sound and taste, steps away from the Colosseum.

A hidden garden in the Monti district. The evening begins by the pool and moves on to a Mediterranean table, a Japanese counter, a room made for listening and a secret karaoke box: one place, many ways to spend the night.

Restaurant · Mediterranean cuisine

Restaurant

Our kitchen speaks Mediterranean: honest ingredients, the rhythm of the seasons, instinctive and recognisable cooking.

Large-catch whole fish, generous cuts, seasonal vegetables, pasta and the dishes of our own culture, retold in the Sanctuary spirit.

A menu made for the middle of the table: to share, taste and discover together.

Sushi bar & Omakase

A sushi bar built around a long counter, with a menu all its own.

Once a month, a handful of seats and an Omakase experience: the chef leads the way, following the catch, the season and the finest cuts.

The most intimate corner of the Sanctuary, essential and devoted to Japan.

Pool Area · Thursday to Sunday, from 6 pm

Sunset by the pool

From Thursday to Sunday, from 6 pm, a generous buffet, prepared down to the last detail, opens the evening.

Come just for an aperitif: a drink, something to taste, friends to meet, and the Sanctuary to discover before dinner and the night.

A sunset ritual around the pool.

Club Room · Sound by Audio Factory

Club Room

A space to live the night through music: an intimate, immersive lounge for meeting, listening and letting the sound carry you.

At its heart, a bespoke high-fidelity sound system designed by Audio Factory, turning every set into a deep, enveloping, almost physical experience.

Here music is back at the centre, to be truly listened to, together. Emerging and international artists, new connections and nights that carry on in a more intimate dimension.

Private karaoke room

Karaoke

A secret room, hidden from the rest of the world. The door closes, the lights dim, the microphone comes on.

A private space, entirely yours.

Inspired by the karaoke boxes of Tokyo, it is a small hideaway devoted to music and sharing: a place to feel free, lose track of time and turn a night among friends into a memory to take home.

Reserve the room

Billiards room

Billiards

A place to be together, have a drink, talk, play and spend the evening without hurry.

At its centre stands a large billiard table, bringing back the simple conviviality of a living room at home.

A warm, intimate and familiar room, where you feel at home and a game is just an excuse to stay together a little longer.
