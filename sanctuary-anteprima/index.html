Our philosophy

«We have designed a space with an ethnic, scenographic and functional style to give people the inspiration to try many different experiences.»

An innovative model that combines music, gathering and wellness.

The Sanctuary was conceived as a multicultural village, somewhere between Tulum, Mexico and Thailand. Each location is different from the others, but the aesthetic of the interiors is essential to all of them.

The concept that defines our places is the beauty of imperfection.

The Sanctuary is a place where ideas, stories and creativity come together.
