Cookie policy

Cookie policy

Information on the use of cookies and similar tools, according to the Guidelines of the Italian Data Protection Authority of 10 June 2021.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves in the browser of its visitors. Some tools, such as the browser's local storage, work in a similar way.

The cookies of this website

This website does not set its own cookies. It does not use profiling cookies, statistics cookies or other tracking tools.

The browser's local storage

When you choose "Accept" or "Decline" in the banner, your browser remembers your choice in its local storage, under the name sanctuary-privacy-v1.

It is only used not to show you the banner again and to load the maps. It contains no personal data and is not sent to us or to third parties. Being a technical tool, it does not require consent.

Third-party services

The map on the location pages is provided by OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation) and loads only after you accept.

When the map is visible, your browser connects to the OpenStreetMap servers, which may receive your IP address, as described in their privacy policy.

The opening videos are hosted on this website: no external video service is used.

How to manage your choices

You can review your choice at any time: the banner will appear again.

You can also set your browser to block cookies: the website will keep working. For everything else, read the privacy notice.
